Description

The Master program in Banking and Finance provides a comprehensive picture of the international business environment while, at the same time, drilling down into specific areas of core relevance.

The Master consists of several short courses which focus mainly on economics, business economics, international relations and international law, providing in-depth understanding of topics such as market dynamics, financial and development finance, investment evaluation, business consulting in public policy and the organization of venture capital funds.

The program aims at meeting the market needs of companies which want to offer a fast-growing career to selected employees as well as the requirements of professionals interested in personal growth or professional development in service companies and financial firms

Learning Objectives

The Master program allows participants to build up new competencies, to improve problem-solving abilities, to acquire knowledge of target market areas and to perfect leadership, creativity and communication skills.

Program

Module 1

International institutions and the challenges of financial stability

  • International Institutional Governance from a Non-Euro Country Perspective
  • Economy of International Financial Institutions
  • European Banking Integration
  • Banking Supervision in the SSM
  • US Market Regulatory Schemes – An Inner Perspective

Module 2

International institutions and the challenges of financial stability

  • Investing in Innovation: the case of VC Investing
  • Money & Payment Systems in International
  • Commercial Law
  • How Multinational Corporations Can Thrive in the New World Order
  • Cost controls in innovative pharmaceutical therapies: the Managed Entry Agreements

Module 3

Communication, privacy, environment in the digital era

  • The Three Main Crisis in the Digital Ecosystem: Truth, Privacy and Free Speech
  • The Role of Marketing and Communications for International Organizations
  • Cases of Financial Institutions Restructuring
  • Climate Change – Financial and Business Risks
  • Monetary Economics

Module 4

Organization and new challenges

  • Public Policy Consulting
  • East-West Digital Virtual Synchronization of Global Reach Power
  • Innovation Ecosystem, Policy, and the Emerging Finance

Internship and Project Work

Internships will give participants the opportunity to work on a project under the supervision of an academic or company tutor in order to gain practical experience in the field. Internship activities will be instrumental to completing a project work which shall be submitted during the final test in order to earn the diploma.

Assessment Criteria

A set of self-assessment tests is provided for each course. Completing the tests is strongly recommended as it is useful for preparing the final examination which students shall attend before a commission (as per Italian DM 270/2004- art. 11 c.7 lett. e).

Payment Methods and Financing

Payment can be made through:

SEPA Direct Debit – Marconi DirectPay

Thanks to the DirectPay direct-debit service, we simplify the process of paying university tuition fees for students. You will no longer need to remember payment deadlines or risk incurring additional costs or late-payment interest in the event of delays.

When an installment is due, the university will send the payment request directly to the bank account indicated and authorized by the student, ensuring an efficient process with no additional costs.

To activate the service, students can access their personal area after completing enrollment and select “Student Services/Tuition Fees/Request SEPA Service Activation.”

By completing the relevant form, the service will be activated, providing a convenient and secure way to manage university tuition payments.

Merit-Based Loan

UniMarconi has partnered with Intesa Sanpaolo to offer “Per Merito,” a solution designed to support students throughout their academic journey and promote greater financial independence during their studies.

For further information, you can consult the Intesa Sanpaolo website directly.

PA 110 e lode

For public-sector employees who enroll through the PA 110 e lode program, the University provides a €330 discount on the enrollment fee for first- and second-level Master's programs. In this case, the fee can be paid in installments, up to a maximum of 4 installments.

The program also provides a contribution of up to 50% of the Master's tuition cost, up to a maximum of €2,500. The contribution is granted only upon completion of the academic program.

In all cases, the payment of contributions to students and the University is subject to the funds allocated annually by the Department of Public Administration and to the actual availability of those funds.
